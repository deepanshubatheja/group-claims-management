# ClaimFlow

ClaimFlow is a desktop app for law firms handling group claims for investors.

## What problem does it solve?

A law firm may work with 15–20 investors. Each investor may have many funds, subfunds and accounts. The firm receives holdings statements, spreadsheets and other documents at different times.

The difficult part is keeping track of who held what, when things changed, who owns the claim rights, and who should pay which share of the legal fees.

ClaimFlow helps bring those records together. It is meant to show changes, point out missing or conflicting information, and support clear, checkable billing calculations.

## What should it do?

- Let staff add documents or entire folders themselves.
- Keep the original documents and record when they were added.
- Read holdings, accounts, funds, subfunds, securities and dates from the documents.
- Compare records from different dates and show what changed.
- Keep security transfers separate from transfers of legal claim rights.
- Flag important conflicts and missing details instead of making guesses.
- Work out each party's share of costs using the agreed billing rules.
- Show where every important figure came from and how each amount was calculated.

## How will staff use it?

1. Open ClaimFlow on their computer.
2. Add the relevant documents.
3. Review the holdings, changes and any issues the app finds.
4. Resolve important questions using the original evidence.
5. Review the billing breakdown and the sources behind it.

The app is intended to be simple to use. Documents are kept on the user's computer. If an optional online AI service is used, any document content sent to it must be handled under a clear permission process. Local storage does not mean AI analysis is always offline.
